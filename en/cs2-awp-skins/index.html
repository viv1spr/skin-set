How to pick a AWP skin

Decide on the color of the whole loadout first: a AWP skin looks better when the knife and gloves share its palette. The Skinset builder locks the skin you pick and fills the other slots for your budget. To save money, go for Field-Tested: Minimal Wear often costs twice as much and the difference is barely visible in game.

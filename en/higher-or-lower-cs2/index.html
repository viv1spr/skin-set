Higher or Lower

Two CS2 skins. You know the price of the first one — guess whether the second costs more or less. Every right answer adds +1 to your streak, a miss starts over. The further you go, the closer the prices.

How to play

  1. The left skin's price is shown, the right one is hidden.
  2. Press Higher or Lower to guess the second skin's price.
  3. Right — the second skin moves left and a new one appears. Wrong — your streak resets.
  4. With every right answer the prices get closer, so it gets harder.

Where do the prices come from?

They're the lowest market prices for the stated wear (Factory New, Field-Tested, etc.), updated every 4 hours — the same prices as in the Skinset builder.

Where is my record saved?

In your browser. Clearing site data or switching devices resets it.

Why does the same skin have different prices?

Wear matters a lot: Factory New is often several times pricier than Battle-Scarred, so the game always shows the wear.

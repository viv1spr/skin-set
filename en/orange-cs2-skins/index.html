How to build a orange CS2 loadout

Balance orange and white: with an AWP and AK-47 Asiimov, take white or black pistols (USP-S Cortex, Glock-18 Bullet Queen). Knife: Asiimov, Fade or Tiger Tooth; gloves: Sport Gloves Omega or Driver Gloves in orange and white. Avoid red next to orange, it warms the set up and kills the clean Asiimov look.

How it works

Skinset doesn't sell skins or open cases. It's a builder: you set a color and a budget, it assembles items that look like one set and sends you to a marketplace to buy them.

01
Pick a color

A preset palette or your own shade via the picker. A second color helps build combos like red & black or white & orange.

02
We compare shades

Each skin in the database has two or three main colors. We convert them into a perceptual color space, where distance matches how the eye sees it, and find the nearest. Skins with stray colors are penalized.

03
Buy piece by piece

Click a slot, inspect the item up close and jump to the marketplace with one button. Lock the items you like and shuffle the rest until the loadout clicks.

FAQ

Why are prices shown as tiers, not exact?

The price of the same skin depends on wear, float, stickers and the marketplace, and changes daily. Tiers from “under $4” to “$1,700+” show the ballpark; the exact price is on the marketplace at checkout.

What do “Spot on”, “Great” and “Close” mean?

How close the skin's main colors are to yours. “Spot on” is a near-perfect match, “Close” fits the overall scheme, “Nearest” is the best available in the budget but not ideal.

How is the budget split?

You set one amount for the whole loadout. Each slot gets a minimum first, then tiers are raised while money lasts: knife and gloves first, weapons follow. If a knife doesn't fit even at minimum, the slot is marked “over budget”.

Can I buy the whole loadout in one click?

Not yet. Every skin is a unique listing, so we link to the marketplace search for each item. A one-cart checkout needs a direct marketplace integration — we're working on it.

Where do the images and names come from?

Item images and names belong to Valve and are used as in any fan catalog. Skinset is not affiliated with Valve and is not an official Counter-Strike service.

Before you buy

Check wear and float

The same skin in Factory New and Battle-Scarred looks different and the price differs several times over. Filter by wear in the marketplace search, and check float and screenshots on expensive items.

Steam trade hold

After buying on a third-party marketplace the item arrives via a Steam trade. You need the mobile authenticator enabled, otherwise there may be a hold of up to a week.

No cases or gambling

Skinset doesn't promote case-opening or betting sites. Only marketplaces where you buy a specific item at a fixed price.

How to pick gloves under $75

  • Go for Field-Tested or Well-Worn. Glove wear is barely visible in game, while Factory New can cost 5–10× more than FT.
  • The most affordable models right now are Hydra Gloves, Driver Gloves, Moto Gloves, Hand Wraps. Sport and Specialist Gloves cost much more even in plain finishes.
  • Gloves set the color of the loadout. Pick them together with the knife: the Skinset builder locks the gloves and matches a knife and weapons in the same palette.

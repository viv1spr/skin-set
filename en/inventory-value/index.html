More about inventory value

How to check your CS2 inventory value

  1. Open your Steam profile and copy the page address, e.g. steamcommunity.com/id/nickname or steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198…
  2. Paste it above and press “Check value”.
  3. In a few seconds you'll see the total value of your CS2 inventory, how many items were priced and which skins are worth the most.

Trade links and vanity names work too, and you can check anyone's inventory as long as it's public.

Where to find your Steam profile link

In the Steam client click your name → Profile, then right-click an empty spot → Copy Page URL. In a browser it's in the address bar. A SteamID64 is 17 digits starting with 7656119. Your trade link is under Inventory → Trade Offers → Who can send me trade offers.

How to make your inventory public

If you see “inventory is private”, go to Steam: Profile → Edit Profile → Privacy Settings and set Inventory to Public (your profile must be public too). Steam takes a couple of minutes to apply it. A public inventory is safe: people can see your items but can't take them without your Steam Guard confirmation.

How the value is calculated

Each item is priced at the lowest current listing on major marketplaces (Lis-Skins and Skinport), by exact wear (Factory New to Battle-Scarred), StatTrak and Souvenir versions. Prices refresh every 5 minutes. Knives, gloves, weapon skins, cases, capsules, stickers, agents, patches and graffiti all count, including items on trade hold.

Why it differs from Steam and other checkers

The Steam Community Market takes a 15% fee and its balance can't be withdrawn, so prices there are usually 15–30% higher than on third-party marketplaces. Instant-sell services pay 10–40% below market. We show the market price: what the same item costs from the cheapest seller right now.

What isn't included

Applied stickers, exact float and rare patterns (Case Hardened blue gems, Doppler phases: Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald are worth several times more) aren't priced separately; such items are valued as the regular version, so the real value can be higher. Use our float calculator to check wear by float.

What next

Once you know your inventory's value, turn it into a matching loadout: in the Skinset builder pick your knife or gloves color and we'll match weapons and an agent in the same palette within your budget.

FAQ

Is it free and safe?

Yes. It's free and needs no Steam login: we only read the public inventory, like any visitor to your profile. No site ever needs your password, Steam Guard code or API key to check a value; if one asks, it's a scam.

Why does it say my inventory is private?

Your Steam privacy settings hide it. Set Inventory to Public under Privacy Settings and wait a couple of minutes.

Can I check someone else's inventory?

Yes, if their profile and inventory are public: paste their Steam profile link.

How often are prices updated?

Marketplace prices refresh every 5 minutes; the inventory itself is cached for 15 minutes.

Are stickers and float included?

Applied stickers and exact float aren't; each item is valued at the lowest price for its wear. Loose stickers in the inventory are counted.

Why weren't all my items priced?

Medals, operation coins, badges and gifts can't be sold, so they have no price. Very rare items with no current listings may also be unpriced.

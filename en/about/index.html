How it works

Pick a color

One or two — say, red and black. Skins are matched by the actual colors of their images, not by name.

Set a budget

One amount for the whole set: knife and gloves get the biggest share, rifles the middle, pistols the rest.

Fine-tune it

Lock what you like, shuffle the rest, choose wear from FN to BS. The set price updates instantly.

Buy or share

Every skin has a marketplace buy button. Copy the set, save it as an image or publish it to the feed.

What Skinset can do

Color matching

We compare every skin's colors to your palette and show the closest matches.

Whole-set budget

The budget is split between slots by importance and the total stays within limits.

Live prices

Prices and wear update every 5 minutes — see the cost of each item and the whole set.

Your inventory

Sign in with Steam and the site finds skins you already own and builds the set around them.

Community sets

Publish your sets, browse and like others, take any set for yourself in one click.

Guides

A blog with ready-made color sets, combos and loadouts for any budget.

FAQ

Is Skinset free?

Yes, completely. We earn from marketplace affiliate links — your price doesn't change.

Where do prices come from?

From major marketplaces, updated every 5 minutes. Exact price and availability are on the marketplace.

Why sign in with Steam?

So the site can see your public CS2 inventory and build a set around skins you already own. You only enter your password on Steam.

Is this an official Valve site?

No, it's a fan project not affiliated with Valve.

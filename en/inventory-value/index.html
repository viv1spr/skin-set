Rate your CS2 inventory

We calculate what your skins are worth and rate your style: how well your knife, gloves and weapons match in color. We'll show which item clashes and what to add so the inventory looks like one set.

or paste a link to any public Steam profile

How the score works

  • Style (55%) — how well the key items (knife, gloves, AK-47, M4, AWP, Desert Eagle, USP-S, Glock-18) share one color palette. Black, white and gray go with everything.
  • Completeness (25%) — how many of the 8 key slots have a skin.
  • Value (20%) — total skin value at current lowest market prices; it grows logarithmically, so a cheap but stylish inventory can beat an expensive mess.

FAQ

Is it safe?

Yes. We only read the public item list through Steam, like any inventory checker. Steam sign-in happens on Steam's official page; we never see your password and never ask for trades or account access.

Why does it say my inventory is private?

Set “Inventory: Public” in your Steam privacy settings. It applies right away, sometimes within a few minutes.

Why is the value different from other sites?

We use the lowest market price for each wear, and the regular-version price for StatTrak™ (hence “≈”). Cases, stickers and agents are not included yet.

Can I rate a friend's or streamer's inventory?

Yes, paste a link to their Steam profile. The profile and inventory must be public.

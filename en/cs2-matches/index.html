FAQ

Where can I watch CS2 matches?

Every live and upcoming match has a stream button — usually the organizer's Twitch or YouTube channel.

How often do scores update?

Live matches refresh about once a minute; schedule and results every few minutes.

Which tournaments are covered?

All pro tournaments tracked by PandaScore: Majors, IEM, BLAST, PGL, ESL Pro League and smaller events. The “Top only” switch keeps S and A tier events.

What do S, A, B, C, D tiers mean?

Tournament level. S is Majors and the biggest events like IEM and BLAST with top teams, A is big tournaments, B–D are regional leagues, qualifiers and online cups.

What do BO1, BO3 and BO5 mean?

Match format: how many maps are played. BO1 is one map, BO3 is first to two maps, BO5 is first to three, usually in grand finals.

What time zone are match times in?

Times are shown in your device's time zone, so there's nothing to convert.

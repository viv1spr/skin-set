How to use it

Float is a number from 0 to 1 set when a skin drops; it never changes. Copy the float from the in-game inspect window or a marketplace listing and paste it above: the calculator shows the wear and how far you are from the next one. Then pick a skin to see the lowest price for each wear and what moving one step up costs.

Wear boundaries

Factory New 0.00–0.07, Minimal Wear 0.07–0.15, Field-Tested 0.15–0.38, Well-Worn 0.38–0.45, Battle-Scarred 0.45–1.00. Many skins have a narrower range: AWP | Asiimov starts at 0.18, so it has no FN or MW, while Doppler and Fade end at 0.08 and only come in FN and MW.

Where to save

The median skin in Factory New costs almost 4× its Field-Tested price. If a step up costs several times more and the skin is dark or matte, go lower: almost nobody will notice in game. More in our guides to CS2 wear and float and pattern.

FAQ

What wear is float 0.15?

Exactly 0.15 is already Field-Tested: Minimal Wear ends at 0.15 and the boundary belongs to the next wear.

Can a skin's float change?

No. Float is set on drop and stays the same however much you play.

Why does a skin have no Factory New?

Its float range is capped. AWP | Asiimov only drops with float 0.18 and above, so it comes in FT, WW and BS only.

Where do I find my skin's float?

Inspect the skin in your CS2 inventory; the float is shown in the inspect window. Marketplaces list it on the item card.
